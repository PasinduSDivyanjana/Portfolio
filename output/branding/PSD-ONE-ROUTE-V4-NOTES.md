## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset: ONE final refined personal monogram logo for Pasindu S Divyanjana, working in information technology and logistics. Compact square symbol for a premium dark portfolio navbar and favicon.
Design concept: "One Route, Two Worlds". A confident geometric P on the left and a D on the right are unified into a single nearly square emblem, with a beautiful generous S-shaped negative-space channel running between them. The P and D are the origin and destination; the S is both the person's middle initial and the seamless route carrying information and physical goods. Make this idea visually immediate, not an explanation pasted onto an arbitrary shape.
New visual execution: a precise upright modular monogram, two complementary broad solid shapes that fit together like engineered components. Top-left P with its open counter, bottom-right D with its counter, the two forms share one continuous fluid S channel that flows diagonally from upper-right to lower-left. Visually calm, strong and timeless. Overall square silhouette, optically balanced, 1:1 proportions. Use a disciplined consistent stroke weight, mostly squared geometry with controlled rounded interior corners. The central S passage must be wide enough to read clearly at small size. No detached dots or extra floating blocks. One small integrated cyan terminal near the bottom end of the route, symbolic of a successful handoff/delivery, belonging to the main shape.
Palette: flat solid pale lavender #C8A9E9 for almost the entire mark, small cyan #8CD5DE accent. Background perfectly uniform solid charcoal-violet #15101D. No shading anywhere.
Composition: square canvas, ONE centered logo occupying about 62% of the canvas width and height, even generous surrounding margins.
Style: exceptional clean flat vector-style identity, designer-crafted, crisp smooth edges. Two or three bold shapes maximum. Subtle meaningful initials integrated as one mark, not three typed letters. Not a chain link, infinity, generic letter B, shield or badge.
No words, name, tagline, labels, presentation sheet, mockup, gradients, highlights, textures, glow, shadows, bevel, 3D, decorative borders, rings, circuit details, shipping trucks, packages, planes, globes, map pins, tiny arrows or extra symbols. Prioritize simplicity and legible negative space.
