# Compact PSD emblem

Created using built-in image generation as a square logo concept for Pasindu S Divyanjana. Lavender and cyan reflect the portfolio palette. The nested letter forms encode the initials; the flowing central S suggests routing and data flow, while the cyan forward chevron suggests delivery and progress. This version has a dark opaque background and has not replaced the website branding.

## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE polished compact square emblem for Pasindu S Divyanjana, an IT and logistics professional. This is a thoughtful personal identity for a dark sophisticated portfolio navbar and favicon. Crucial: the emblem silhouette must be approximately as wide as it is tall, 1:1 aspect ratio. NO horizontal wordmark and NO three letters side by side. Design a single original geometric monogram symbol, like an elegantly engineered routing tile. A bold upright P is the main immediately legible shape; a rounded outer D-shaped boundary shares its upright stem; an S-shaped continuous negative-space channel flows through the two inner chambers. This subtly encodes P S D without sacrificing simplicity. The negative-space route connects one point to another through the mark and ends in ONE restrained rightward directional notch: connected data flow for IT, routing and progress for logistics. No extra illustrated symbols. Use an unusually simple, memorable silhouette, consistent thick strokes, softly rounded outside corners, a few intentional precise angular cuts inside, optical balance, and strong readable negative space. Premium flat vector logo, minimal architectural design. Pale lavender #C8A9E9 main shape with a single small integrated cyan #8CD5DE connector segment, not scattered accents. Place the single symbol centered at about 60% of the square canvas width and 60% of the square canvas height, ample perfectly balanced margins. Solid uniform very dark charcoal-violet background #15101D, fully opaque, no transparency, no checkerboard. Absolutely crisp smooth clean edges, no distress or grain, no gradients, no lighting, no glow, no bevel or 3D, no shadows. No words, no labels, no tagline, no separate letter text beneath, no design sheet, no mockup, no orbit rings, no trucks, no planes, no code brackets, no circuit board detail. Just ONE compact square symbol.
