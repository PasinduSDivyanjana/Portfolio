## Final generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a thoughtful new compact personal identity symbol for Pasindu S Divyanjana, a professional in IT and logistics. ONE finished logo only, no presentation board.
Concept "The Connected Path": integrate P, S and D into ONE upright square emblem. Two substantial interlocking rounded geometric forms suggest a P in the upper left and a D in the lower right; the clean continuous negative space BETWEEN them traces a clearly intentional S-shaped route from top to bottom. This S-shaped passage is the essential idea: information and shipments traveling through a coordinated system. The route must remain OPEN, generous, readable, and elegant. A single small cyan rectangular section incorporated into one lavender form symbolizes a handoff between digital and physical operations. Not an extra floating icon.
Explore a sophisticated Swiss modernist negative-space monogram: compact, perfectly balanced, solid flat shapes, deliberate geometric construction, broad strokes, restrained softly squared corners. Make it look like an original designer-crafted personal mark, not a generic chain link or infinity sign. The overall silhouette should be nearly square and upright, not elongated, not diagonal. It should still be recognizable as a solid silhouette at favicon size. Avoid trying to spell all three letters separately; the negative space S is the clever detail.
Palette: predominantly solid pale lavender #C8A9E9 with ONE solid cyan #8CD5DE inset segment. Background solid charcoal violet #15101D, fully opaque.
Centered on a square canvas, symbol 58% canvas width and height, large balanced margins. Crisp precise vector-like edges, visually flat. No text, no tagline, no letters outside the emblem, no gradients, no shadows, no glow, no 3D, no texture, no tiny lines, no circuit boards, no arrows, no delivery trucks, no globe, no boxes, no surrounding border or badge. Deliver ONE clean memorable compact emblem.
